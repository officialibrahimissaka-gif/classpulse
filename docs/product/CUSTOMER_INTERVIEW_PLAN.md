## 45-minute core guide

**Consent/context, 5 minutes**

1. May we take notes? May we record separately if useful? Which information must not be retained/shared?
2. What do you own, which legal entities/sites/countries and workforce types are involved, and who makes the final operational and spending decisions?
3. Which systems/services are authoritative today? What was your last comparable software/service purchase and how was it approved?

**Last real workflow, 15 minutes**

4. Walk through the last completed start, payroll-input cycle, supervisor time approval or employee request. Begin with the triggering event; who did each step?
5. If permitted, show a redacted tracker, timestamp sequence or synthetic reconstruction. Which source won when records disagreed? Where was data entered twice?
6. What specifically departed from plan? Who noticed, how late, and who resolved it? What remained unresolved?
7. How many similar events occurred in the last two completed cycles or recent 90-day period? What is the denominator? Is this tracked or recalled?
8. Show a successful case. When is your current process entirely adequate? Who believes this is not a problem?

**Cost and alternatives, 10 minutes**

9. What actual staff time, adjustment, delay, invoice/fee effect or dispute resulted? Which record supports it and who can verify the calculation?
10. Which consequences were preventable by coordination software, versus worker choice, client decisions, government processing, staffing capacity or provider calculation?
11. What have you tried in the existing HRMS/ATS, a spreadsheet or a provider service? Was it configured by someone competent? What stopped adoption or made it work?
12. What does the current alternative cost, including implementation/internal effort? Would another system remove a cost or simply add one? What duplication is acceptable?

**Buyer, willingness to pay and delivery, 10 minutes**

13. Who controls this budget and signs an evaluation? What evidence or competing priority would block it?
14. What happened when you last paid to solve a similar issue? Ask for a redacted quote/order pattern if volunteered, not a confidential contract dump.
15. Which systems, data permissions, client participation, languages/devices, security and provider approvals are mandatory before a safe evaluation?
16. What specific outcome would justify a bounded paid evaluation? Who would own it, by what decision date, and how would you know it failed?
17. Could your procurement accept a Ghana seller and GHS billing, or require another seller/currency/invoice/tax arrangement? Founder’s Paystack account does not answer buyer procurement requirements.
18. What next action can we agree: second stakeholder meeting, redacted baseline, incumbent configuration test, or a concrete price/scope discussion with the budget owner?

**Close, 5 minutes:** read back the observed event, cost/uncertainties and counterexample; ask what we misunderstood, permission for follow-up and approved retention/deletion of research notes. Do not present the prototype until the current workflow is documented. A screenshot of the existing agency prototype is not a new full-HRMS promise.

## Workflow probes

| Problem | Specific probes | Do not assume |
|---|---|---|
| P1 agency post-offer | When is the fee contractually earned/invoiced? Who owns client/candidate tasks after acceptance? Planned vs actual starts and reasons? What can the ATS already automate? | Every late start loses a fee or the agency controls the delay |
| P2 payroll inputs | What is your actual pay period/cutoff? Which inputs arrive approved vs disputed? What does provider acceptance/rejection mean? | Monthly pay, one UAE rule set, or an export equals payment |
| P3 site time/assignment | Follow a changed shift/site/overtime entry to approval and payroll; who can dispute/correct it and on what evidence? | New biometric hardware, location surveillance or a full scheduler is required |
| P4 international readiness | Which authorized party collects/checks each necessary status? What is employer decision vs provider/government decision? | Checklist completion is legal eligibility, or storing identity files is necessary |
| P5 SME requests | Trace one leave/pay-query/change from request to closure and payroll impact; show a successful self-service case | Every SME needs a replacement HRMS |

## Cost capture and willingness-to-pay evidence

Calculate only from customer-supplied inputs: attributable rework hours × disclosed loaded hourly cost, plus independently evidenced non-overlapping costs. If a rate or preventable share is estimated, label it. Keep recoverable delay, permanent loss and labor separate. Do not invent a legal penalty or multiply one story into a UAE market total.

Evidence ladder: opinion → past spend/actual workaround → named buyer and procurement path → specific conditional paid-evaluation commitment → approved collected payment → renewal. Only the later steps establish commercial behavior. “Would you pay for AI HR?” and free trial activation are not primary validation.

After facts and incumbent tests, discuss a specific founder-approved scope/price/currency consistently for a cohort. Record offered/accepted/rejected terms and reason. Existing GHS price anchors are hypotheses, not the default answer to every interview. No payment is collected through the unsafe/unapproved prototype; conditional commitments are not cash or MRR.

## Research record and synthesis

For each organization store research ID, primary stratum/tags, role/date, observed event, recurrence denominator, safe artifact reference/permission, actual vs estimated cost, alternatives/plan/configuration, buyer/budget path, participation/data constraints, offered price/currency, commitment status, contradictory evidence, next step/owner and note retention decision. Use restricted minimal contact data; no employee names, passports, medical/bank files or raw private inbox exports.

Separate direct observation from recollection and interpretation. Anonymize summaries by organization ID. Review weekly; code P1–P5, preserve counterexamples and update the [validation scorecard](MARKET_RESEARCH.md#problem-validation-scorecard). No quotes, company names or purported interview results have been invented.
