## What pricing can and cannot tell us

Example [J], **not a quote or market average**: ten Manatal Professional licenses at the annual displayed rate imply USD 150/month equivalent, USD 1,800/year before other scope/cost considerations. The 15-job/account ceiling may make that plan unsuitable. This is the **incumbent ATS budget**, not evidence an agency would pay the same again for a handoff add-on.

Internal recruiter seats, all employees/administrators, active contractors, EOR employees and organization fees are different denominators. Software, payroll processing, legal-employer service and employment costs are different bundles. Obtain comparable quotes for (a) agency with ten internal users and client participants, (b) 100-worker/three-site employer keeping its payroll provider, and (c) multi-entity international-hiring employer. These are synthetic comparison cases, not observed customer averages.

Record edition, geography/currency, annual commitment vs monthly payment, renewal rate, minimums, external users, storage, messaging/AI credits, SSO/API, onboarding/import/training, connector/partner work, support and exit export. Do not convert currencies without a dated explicit rate or compare promotional first-year rates with undiscounted renewals. Existing proposed GHS prices are founder experiments, not customer willingness to pay.

## Fair native-workflow and onboarding comparison

No onboarding experience was tested in this task. Trial/demo CTAs prove availability of a path, not implementation time, service quality or UAE suitability.

After interviews, give two relevant incumbents the same synthetic case: import 20 minimal references, configure the actual owner/approval/task handoff, invite one restricted external participant, simulate a missing item/date change, inspect audit/source-to-metric trace and export/exit. Observe purchase-to-access, import/configuration effort, required edition/partner, external-user friction, error recovery and full cost. Separate a sales demonstration from researcher-operated testing. Do not register/pay/contact vendors automatically through this plan.

**Competitive recommendation [H/J]:** seek a specific, artifact-backed coordination failure that survives competent incumbent setup. If two native tests solve it sufficiently, the agency add-on hypothesis should lose priority—even though a prototype already exists. Payroll-input coordination and international-hiring metadata must face the same test against suites and service providers.
