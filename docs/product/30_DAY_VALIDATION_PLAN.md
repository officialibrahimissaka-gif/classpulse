## Weekly checkpoint and experiment log

Each Friday-equivalent research checkpoint records: approached/consenting/booked/completed organizations by stratum, roles interviewed, recurring cases and denominators, artifacts with permission, documented preventable cost, buyer meetings, incumbent tests, proposed price/currency and conditional commitments/rejections. Record staff time and recruitment source to reveal founder-network bias. Do not claim response/conversion rates without denominators or representative UAE prevalence from this purposive sample.

Keep an evidence register with research ID, source/date, segment, event, supporting/contradicting observation, direct vs recalled data, safe artifact reference, confidentiality/retention decision, buyer, current spend, specific next commitment and responsible researcher. Public review evidence is tagged R, vendor claims V and founder statements F; none substitutes for independent customer workflow evidence.

## Day-30 decision checklist [J]

Advance to a bounded MVP decision only when the **same selected workflow/segment** has:

- Five independent recurring cases with observed/reconstructable handoffs and named accountable owner; three lawful redacted/synthetic process artifacts.
- A material, attributable and potentially preventable cost mechanism with honest unknowns; include successful counterexamples and exclude external uncontrollable causes.
- Two fair incumbent tests demonstrating a meaningful remaining gap at comparable scope/TCO; stop or narrow if native setup suffices.
- Named buyer/procurement path and two specific conditional paid-evaluation commitments including scope, offered price/currency, outcome, participating roles and decision date. A nonbinding compliment, free trial or test payment does not qualify as collected revenue.
- Repeatable authorized data/participant access and plausible delivery/support economics; critical legal/privacy/security constraints identified with owners, not dismissed by the score.

Any no-buyer/no-gap/no-lawful-data result or unmanageable responsibility requirement can veto advance. Fewer cases/commitments means targeted extension or stop, not fabricated confidence. Actual payment and live customer operation require the separate existing release/legal/billing gates; discovery success does not set production to GO.
