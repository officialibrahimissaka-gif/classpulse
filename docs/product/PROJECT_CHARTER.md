# Global HR platform — discovery charter

2 November 2026 · Stage D01: discovery and revalidation · Decision owner: founder.

The repository is not empty. Baseline `7f7decb` contains a working ClassPulse agency placement-to-start prototype, requirements/architecture, nine migrations, launch preparation and recorded 56-test evidence. Earlier discovery files at the repository root were explicitly provisional and often unable to retrieve live sources. They do not establish customer demand. Existing production readiness remains **NO-GO**.

This charter establishes the requested discovery foundation under `docs/product/`, with newly retrieved sources and an explicit choice among segments. Preserve existing code, schema/history and prior evidence. Do not implement the queued import-provenance slice, add features, reset the repository, launch, charge cards or contact prospects as part of this task. The existing prototype can be a synthetic demonstration **after** problem interviews; sunk development cost must not select the market. This discovery charter governs the current research activity; the [engineering charter](../../PROJECT_CHARTER.md) and release checklist retain implementation/security constraints.

## Vision and objectives

**Vision [H]:** a modular global people-and-workforce operations platform, beginning with one demonstrably valuable UAE workflow and expanding only when customer evidence supports adjacent modules and jurisdictions. The eventual vision includes HR records/employee experience, recruitment, time/leave/workforce coordination, payroll-related operations and multi-entity reporting. It is a direction, not an approved all-in-one MVP.

Discovery objectives:

1. Identify a recurring, costly, software-addressable problem with a named operator and economic buyer.
2. Compare UAE SME employers, permanent recruitment, temporary staffing, international-hiring employers, construction, hospitality and logistics; distinguish employment model before industry labels.
3. Establish what current systems/services already solve, their like-for-like total cost and the remaining gap.
4. Test actual budget, purchase process, authorized data access, external-party participation and procurement constraints.
5. Decide one bounded MVP direction—or narrow/stop—using evidence, without confusing a prototype, vendor claim or interview compliment with validation.

## Target markets and boundaries

UAE first; tag establishment authority/regime, entities/sites, workforce types, current provider, country mix and decision rights. “SME” is a discovery label until a specific official definition is verified; no statutory employee threshold is asserted. International expansion is a later customer-pull decision requiring separate country/provider/privacy validation, not an assumption that UAE rules travel unchanged.

Investigate workflow coordination before replacing HRMS/ATS/payroll systems. Do not propose payroll calculations, government submissions, immigration/eligibility decisions, employer-of-record services, worker supply or passport/medical/bank-file storage without a separately approved customer/legal/security case. Human employer/provider decisions remain distinct from checklist completion. No universal compliance guarantee or AI hiring claim is proposed.

## Success criteria and decision gates [J]

These are planning thresholds, not evidence already achieved or statistically representative samples:

- Target 18 independent organizations across the comparison strata, with up to three additional interviews in the leading segment; six role follow-ups count as conversations, not new organizations.
- For the selected **same workflow/segment**, document at least five independent recurring cases, three lawful redacted/synthetic process artifacts and a named buyer/cost mechanism. Do not pool unrelated staffing and employer problems to pass the gate.
- Run two fair incumbent/native-configuration tests and record counterexamples as well as failures.
- Obtain two specific conditional paid-evaluation commitments from budget owners with scope, offered currency/price, outcome and decision date. They are not purchases; actual approved collected payment is needed before claiming demonstrated willingness to pay.
- Identify a repeatable authorized data path, participating users, critical legal/security/procurement dependencies and realistic delivery/support effort.
- Produce an evidence-backed advance/narrow/stop decision by Day 30. A decision to investigate/build is **not production release approval**. If evidence is insufficient, extend targeted research or stop; do not invent scores to meet a date.

## Ownership, resources and governance

Founder owns recruiting access, interviews, buyer/price conversations and prioritization. Product/business analysis owns consistent scripts, evidence quality, competitive tests and synthesis. Payroll/HR/operations practitioners and qualified legal/security advisers validate relevant assumptions; no named advisers or commitments have been supplied. Plan roughly 10–12 founder hours/week as an explicit workload estimate, adjusted to actual availability.

Use [evidence labels](MARKET_RESEARCH.md#evidence-standard), dated sources and an account-level research register. Store minimal business contact metadata; request no sensitive employee documents. Obtain permission for recording and artifacts; agree research retention/access handling. No outreach is sent by these documents.

Deliverables: this charter, market/segment/problem analysis, competitor matrix, interview plan, three MVP options with scorecards, 30-day plan and current development status. New feature implementation remains paused pending a founder-reviewed discovery decision and then the normal engineering/release gates.
