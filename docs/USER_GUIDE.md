# R1 user guide

1. **Set up a company.** Production sign-in uses the configured verified identity provider. Enter the company’s actual country, employment authority, IANA time zone, currency, and reviewed retention schedule. This attestation does not certify compliance or enable payroll. A trial has 5 internal seats, 25 active placements, and one entity for 14 days.
2. **Create client references.** Open Clients and add a stable client code. Assign the correct legal entity. Candidate references and employee identities are separate.
3. **Record an accepted placement.** Enter the existing ATS/source system, distinct candidate and placement source references, acceptance date and planned first day. No email identity merging or document uploads occur. The current published checklist is copied into the placement.
4. **Coordinate tasks.** Complete with a necessary evidence reference, assign internal ownership, or invite a client to explicitly selected tasks and optional fields. Invitees sign in with the invited verified email. Client users cannot waive tasks, review readiness, or confirm starts. Revoking a participant invalidates existing sessions.
5. **Handle exceptions.** Open a categorized blocker; record a resolution before readiness. Authorized agency users may waive unfinished tasks with a reason. Avoid passports, health/bank information, or unnecessary personal data in references and reasons.
6. **Make human decisions.** Required tasks must be completed or waived and exceptions resolved before readiness. Record readiness separately from actual start. Actual start must be between acceptance and today in the placement’s time zone. Rescheduling preserves the original plan; cancellation/start release capacity. Reopening reserves capacity and preserves the earlier transition history.
7. **Import CSV.** Download the sample, select the legal entity/source system, upload up to 1,000 rows/10 MiB, review errors, then commit. Existing source references with conflicting values cause rollback; unchanged rows are skipped. The whole commit fits current capacity or nothing is imported.
8. **Inspect reports.** Filter dates, client and legal entity; KPI cards open server-defined source cohorts. Confirmed-start counts use actual start dates; on-time rate and delay use the original planned-date cohort. Overdue work is a current snapshot, not a reconstructed historic baseline. No-data rates/medians show a dash. Save private/team filter views, print, or generate PDF/CSV/XLSX through the worker. Downloads require current access and expire after seven days.
9. **Manage modules and plans.** Read-only modules retain records and allow source reads/exports within the access window. Turning CRM read-only requires confirming dependent Agency starts. Reactivation preserves data. Sensitive controls require fresh MFA. Plan requests do not charge or activate paid service; an operator must verify an accepted commercial order.
10. **Administer privacy.** Verify the requester before recording an erasure request; enter a reviewed deadline and verification reference. A separate operator reviews legal holds and retention disposition before erasure. Any active legal hold blocks the conservative cleanup workflow. Operational and backup/provider obligations remain subject to independent review.

The local developer identity adapter deliberately cannot satisfy fresh MFA. Production reauthentication returns to the workspace with a newly checked session; the provider/pool controls must pass deployment review.

## Pricing and billing preview

Open `/pricing` after starting the app to review proposed GHS plan prices, implemented module scope and limits. Prices are explicitly unvalidated experiments; the page does not collect a card or sell a subscription. The evaluation link returns to the actual sign-in/setup flow.

An owner with tenant-scoped billing permission can open `/billing` to refresh reconciled Paystack **test** observations. Empty results are expected until a separate authorized test operator verifies a provider reference. Recruiters and client participants cannot access these records. Payment observations are not invoices or proof of settled revenue; no payment here changes module or plan eligibility. Continue to use the existing reviewed commercial-order process for plan requests, and do not activate live customer data while the release is NO-GO.
