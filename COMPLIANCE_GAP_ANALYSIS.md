# Phase 6 compliance gap analysis

**Disposition: BLOCKED for production.** This is a control-to-requirement gap review, not legal advice or independent legal approval. The product is R1 agency coordination, not payroll, immigration, an employer of record or employment-law automation. Country/authority/retention fields are customer attestations; configuration does not establish statutory compliance.

The approved architecture distinguishes mainland/MOHRE, ordinary free-zone arrangements, DIFC and ADGM and their potentially different employment/data-protection applicability. Actual entities, processing purposes, legal roles, hosting/providers/support locations and customer contracts have not been legally assessed. `UAE_COMPLIANCE_RESEARCH.md` remains the research/applicability register; this audit did not turn its outstanding legal questions into validated production rules. No new statutory deadlines, retention durations, payroll formulas, market figures or legal claims were invented.

## Employment/payroll and financial scope

No WPS submission, wage payment, leave accrual, gratuity/end-of-service, overtime, payslip, tax, bank transfer or payroll rounding engine is implemented. These validations are **N/A for R1**, and the features remain unavailable. N/A does not mean any UAE regime’s payroll requirements passed. Introducing such a module needs current official-source validation, jurisdiction-specific adapters, independent calculation examples and separate release review.

Commercial-order state/capacity is in scope: changed retries and reused references now fail; exact retries do not duplicate processing; effective-date ordering, cancellation exit windows and retained over-capacity data are locally tested. No amount, price, refund, proration, money rounding or payment reconciliation is calculated by the application.

## Required sign-offs

Before activating production: named controller/processor/legal applicability and contractual approval; independent security/IAM/provider review; complete privacy/retention/operator procedures; managed restore and incident exercises; manual accessibility/user acceptance and missing R1 functional clauses. No sign-off is supplied or inferred by this document or by successful unit tests.
