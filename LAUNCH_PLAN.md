## Gate-based launch sequence

No fixed calendar launch date is promised. These are proposed work packages after dependencies close, not a statement a four-week schedule is achievable.

1. **Discovery now — founder/product:** assemble a permissioned 40-account research list and interview cases, including satisfied ATS users; meet existing gate of five independent recurring cases, three lawful/redacted artifacts and two specific paid-evaluation commitments. Check foreign-seller/GHS procurement objections explicitly. No outreach is sent without separate instruction and applicable channel review.
2. **Synthetic staging — platform/security:** connect chosen AWS account/domain and test-only Paystack secret through secure settings; validate infrastructure plan/costs, regional patches and roles, deploy dark staging with synthetic data, then exercise identity/mail/storage/restore/monitoring. Resolve architecture gaps before customer-data staging. No live key is needed for this stage.
3. **Close launch blockers — accountable owners:** implement/retest remaining R1 clauses, live billing lifecycle/invoice controls and operational/privacy gaps; complete legal/accountant/security reviews. Use the exact-code protected release record; do not just set NO_GO to GO.
4. **Three design-partner pilots — founder/CS:** sign bounded scope/terms after approval, agree the actual GHS pilot/continuation price, onboard minimal authorized data, observe one cohort and measure support effort/value. Payment processing requires approved live-provider controls; no collection occurs beforehand.
5. **First ten — founder-led sales:** only expand after usable activation and first paid continuations. Improve one workflow/message and onboarding issue per cohort; do not add broad HRMS/payroll to rescue an unfocused sale.
6. **Public campaign — growth/release owners:** after repeatable collected revenue/retention evidence, publish reviewed workflow page/demo/pricing, opt-in checklist/content and permissioned customer story. Pilot language and production claims must match actual evidence. Paid acquisition waits for attributable conversion and positive contribution assumptions.

Planning funnel: 60 qualified accounts →30 attended demos →15 activated/started evaluations →10 paying accounts. These 50%/50%/66.7% stage conversions are hypotheses, not industry benchmarks or a forecast. The initial 40 research accounts are a discovery subset, not an additional 40 leads. Use account/cohort maturity rules in REVENUE_MODEL.md. Revise outreach volume only after observed reply/qualification rates and lawful capacity are known.

Stop/pivot criteria: repeated evidence native ATS configuration already solves the job; no accountable budget owner; foreign-seller/GHS payment requirements unacceptable; no paid commitment after a pre-agreed discovery batch; support/infrastructure cost exceeds sustainable contribution; any unclosed critical/high security/integrity/financial blocker. Record counterexamples and losses. Do not count free or test accounts toward ten paying customers.
