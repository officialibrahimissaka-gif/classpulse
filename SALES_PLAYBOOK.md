# Sales playbook

2 November 2026. Draft founder-led motion; no outreach sent and no customer claims made. All funnel counts are planning assumptions. Production and legal gates precede paid customer activation.

## Ideal customer profile and qualification

Start with independent UAE permanent-recruitment agencies using an existing ATS, a named coordination owner and repeated accepted offers awaiting start. Test a working-size hypothesis of 5–30 internal coordinators/recruiters, not an established segment fact. Buyer: owner/operations head; champion: placement lead. Verify lawful access to client/candidate references and whether procurement can accept a Ghana seller and GHS invoices. Disqualify temporary staffing/labor supply/payroll needs, agencies already satisfied with native ATS handoffs, firms requiring unavailable integrations/Arabic/document processing and firms lacking buyer ownership.

Positioning hypothesis: “A shared operating checklist for accepted placements, with clear agency/client ownership and human readiness decisions before the first day.” Differentiation is the narrow workflow plus field-scoped client participation and source-linked operational reporting. Do not claim unique features, superior ROI or competitor inability without current hands-on evidence. Compare against ATS configuration, spreadsheets plus email, recruitment CRM workflows and human managed service. Competitor pricing/claims remain as qualified in COMPETITOR_ANALYSIS.md; no new current vendor facts were independently established in this phase.

## Discovery questions and objection handling

Ask for the last accepted placement with a handoff problem: who owned it, what information was missing, which date changed, what was preventable and what evidence supports the actual cost? Observe the current ATS workflow and a successful counterexample. Ask who signs and pays, what security/data/FX procurement conditions apply and what a paid continuation must demonstrate. If the existing ATS can solve it, document that result and stop selling the add-on. If asked for payroll, permits or native integration, state the current exclusion clearly. Price objections trigger evidence/budget discussion, not invented discounts or savings.

## Ten-minute synthetic demo

1. Minute 0–1: state agency workflow and exclusions, use only a synthetic tenant; introduce owner/recruiter/client roles.
2. Minute 1–3: enter an accepted placement beside an existing client, show original/current start date and versioned checklist/owner.
3. Minute 3–5: show a missing required task/open exception blocking readiness, resolve with evidence reference or reviewed waiver; keep human decisions explicit.
4. Minute 5–6: show a scoped client view that cannot read another client or unshared field; revoke a synthetic grant.
5. Minute 6–8: mark ready, independently confirm actual start and drill from the on-time metric to its exact cohort. Explain small/empty denominators.
6. Minute 8–9: generate/download an authorized report with the worker, show CSV import validation and retained module-off access. Never pretend a queued report is finished.
7. Minute 9–10: show proposed GHS pricing and test-only billing labels; ask for a bounded evaluation decision after production gates. No live card or unreviewed customer data.

## Outreach drafts — not sent

Use only lawful/permissioned contacts, approved channel and applicable outreach review. Record opt-out immediately; no scraped bulk list or unsolicited sensitive attachments. Proposed sequence caps: introduction, one useful follow-up, close-the-loop; stop upon refusal/opt-out.

**Introduction:** “Hello [name], I’m researching how permanent-recruitment agencies coordinate accepted offers until the first day. Does your team encounter handoffs that your existing ATS does not handle well? If this is your responsibility, would a short conversation about one recent case be useful? Happy to stop if not relevant.”

**Follow-up, after an appropriate reviewed interval:** “One question from our workflow research: who owns incomplete client tasks once an offer is accepted? I can share a simple checklist outline if helpful. No need to send candidate documents.”

**Close:** “I’ll close this thread unless the accepted-placement workflow is a priority. If another time is better, let me know. I won’t add you to a mailing list.”

After consent/discovery, invite a synthetic demo with exact exclusions and release status. Do not offer live customer access before approval. Partner conversations with ATS implementers may test referrals after direct pain validation; no partnership, commission or integration access exists yet.

## Account register and review

Track account source/consent, ICP fit, buyer/champion, incident/artifact references, incumbent test, security/procurement/FX blockers, offered price/currency, demo/evaluation dates, paid commitment versus collected cash, activation, outcome and loss reason. Access-restrict personal contact data and apply reviewed retention. Weekly founder review examines denominators, lost deals and support cost, not only pipeline optimism. No email, campaign or sales presentation has been sent on the user's behalf.
