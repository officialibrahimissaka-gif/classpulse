## Regulatory requirement records

Each REG record includes source, effective-date evidence, business/data/validation/reporting behavior and outstanding legal review. “Reporting” means operational evidence or a statutory report only where actually established; no unverified government filing is invented. Proposed implementation uses GLOB-003 and existing REQ-F042/F043/TEST-F042/F043; none is encoded or activated.

### REG-AE-001 — Scope, permits and recruitment role

- **Jurisdiction/applicability:** L1-scoped private-sector employers, workers and recruitment/mediation activity; exclusions and special authority regimes require confirmation.
- **Official source:** L1 Articles 3 and 6. Article 6 says recruitment/mediation requires a Ministry license and prohibits employer collection of recruitment/employment fees from workers, directly or indirectly.
- **Effective date:** base law 2 February 2022; applicable amendments/implementing instruments must be checked for the customer/event.
- **Business behavior:** distinguish offer acceptance, work permit, residency and actual start; retain the authorized owner's status/evidence. A checklist cannot declare a permit valid or grant a recruitment license.
- **Data requirements:** entity/authority, activity/worker category, authorized agency/employer/provider, milestone evidence reference and reviewer; no identity-document upload in R1.
- **Validation requirements:** explicit supported regime/role, expiring evidence, no automated worker-fee deduction/service charge or eligibility approval; review conflicts rather than infer approval.
- **Reporting requirements:** audit ownership and changes; show unknown/pending/confirmed-by-authority separately. No government submission is implemented.
- **Outstanding legal review:** current agency licensing, temporary staffing/labor supply distinction, authority/free-zone permit rules and contractual role of each participant.

### REG-AE-002 — Contracts and employment-file records

- **Jurisdiction/applicability:** employees within L1, not a universal rule for every candidate/free-zone worker.
- **Official source:** L1 Article 8 addresses employment contracts/copies and definite periods; Article 13(1) requires worker-file retention not less than two years from end of service and refers to Ministry conditions/procedures.
- **Effective date:** base 2 February 2022; customer-applicable amendment and Ministry retention details pending.
- **Business behavior:** version approved contract terms and delivery evidence; maintain an approved employee-file retention floor for covered records. Do not keep every candidate record for two years solely by analogy.
- **Data requirements:** employment/contract version, parties, dates, wage currency/type, delivery/acceptance references, termination date, retention class and hold.
- **Validation requirements:** required contract data checked against reviewed applicable form; retention engine prevents premature deletion of covered employee files; no invented maximum contract term.
- **Reporting requirements:** missing required records, contract version/delivery and retention/deletion/hold evidence; no unverified mandatory filing assumed.
- **Outstanding legal review:** current prescribed forms, languages/signatures/evidence, particular record classes and interactions with privacy/other limitation periods.

### REG-AE-003 — Wage terms, due dates and currency

- **Jurisdiction/applicability:** L1-covered employment and relevant Ministry payment system requirements.
- **Official source:** L1 Article 22 requires payment on due dates under Ministry-approved systems; wages in AED or another currency agreed in the employment contract. L11 describes WPS information/fund flow.
- **Effective date:** L1 base 2 February 2022; L11 guidance updated 24 February 2026. Current operative WPS resolutions/deadlines/exemptions unverified.
- **Business behavior:** capture agreed currency/pay period/due date, preserve approval/export/provider/payment/reconciliation states separately. File generation cannot mark salary paid.
- **Data requirements:** employer/provider IDs, worker references, contract currency, amount/type, period, due date, approved batch/format version, provider acknowledgment and reconciliation references.
- **Validation requirements:** no mixed-currency aggregate; require reviewed provider format and coverage; block unsupported finalization. No guessed WPS tolerance, employee coverage percentage or fine.
- **Reporting requirements:** batch totals, due/pending/rejected/accepted/reconciled exceptions and provider evidence. Statutory formats only after provider/authority acceptance review.
- **Outstanding legal review:** current MOHRE instruments, authority/employer coverage, exceptions, due-date rules, provider agreements/identifiers and permitted currency behavior within a particular payment channel.

### REG-AE-004 — Working hours, breaks and related pay inputs

- **Jurisdiction/applicability:** L1-covered workers, subject to category/sector exceptions and executive rules.
- **Official source:** L1 Article 17 states maximum normal hours of eight per day or 48 per week and delegates categories/Ramadan detail; related rest/overtime provisions require full adapter review.
- **Effective date:** base 2 February 2022; relevant current implementing/category rules unresolved.
- **Business behavior:** record actual time, approvals, work calendar and exception category; do not treat the base-hour statement as a complete overtime or shift-compliance engine.
- **Data requirements:** local work date/timezone, recorded minutes, approved corrections, worker category, calendar/rule version and source evidence.
- **Validation requirements:** review sector, work pattern, applicable breaks/rest/overtime/holiday/Ramadan rules before calculations; test date boundaries and actual vs planned time.
- **Reporting requirements:** approved hours, missing/conflicting entries and versioned exceptions; legal compliance output remains unavailable without reviewed coverage.
- **Outstanding legal review:** construction/healthcare/hospitality/logistics exceptions, overtime bases/caps, shift patterns, remote work, Ramadan and executive regulations.

### REG-AE-005 — Leave entitlements and units

- **Jurisdiction/applicability:** L1-covered employment with type/service qualifications.
- **Official source:** L1 Article 29 includes 30 days annual leave per extended service year and two days per month where service is more than six months and less than one year; part-time and remaining-service provisions qualify the general rule. Other leave types have distinct provisions.
- **Effective date:** base 2 February 2022; implementing/detail and amendment review pending.
- **Business behavior:** leave ledger must distinguish calendar/working days, service periods, accrual, approval and legally reviewed carry-forward/settlement. Do not apply the quoted figure to DIFC/ADGM or all work patterns.
- **Data requirements:** service dates, employment pattern, policy/year/calendar, unit, ledger entries and approved absence; medical evidence S3 and minimized when later supported.
- **Validation requirements:** reviewed edge-case fixtures for partial service, part-time, absence, public holidays, contract improvement and termination; no unreviewed legal formula in production.
- **Reporting requirements:** balance/consumption/reservations with units, policy version and adjustment evidence.
- **Outstanding legal review:** sick/maternity/parental/bereavement/study and other leave, applicable wage basis, accrual/rounding, carry-forward and executive rules.

### REG-AE-006 — Termination, settlement and end-of-service benefits

- **Jurisdiction/applicability:** L1-covered employment; Article 51 specifically distinguishes national/foreign full-time workers and relevant scheme/pension cases.
- **Official source:** L1 Article 43 sets written notice boundaries for its general case; Article 51 describes qualifying foreign full-time gratuity on basic wage, with 21 days for each first-five-year year and 30 thereafter, qualifications and exclusions; Article 53 requires covered end-of-contract entitlements within 14 days.
- **Effective date:** base 2 February 2022; current scheme/exception/implementing amendments require verification.
- **Business behavior:** preserve termination reason/effective date/notice/approval; separate settlement calculation, approval, due date and payment. Do not apply one formula across nationalities, schemes or jurisdictions.
- **Data requirements:** employment history, basic wage/version, unpaid periods, contract/notice, nationality only where necessary for reviewed rule, pension/alternative-scheme participation, balance and payment evidence.
- **Validation requirements:** reviewed rule/coverage required; test threshold service, partial years, unpaid periods, scheme participation, caps/deductions and notice exceptions. No autonomous termination decision.
- **Reporting requirements:** explainable calculation/version, due/paid/reconciled status and outstanding exceptions, not a legal guarantee.
- **Outstanding legal review:** alternative savings/pension systems, lawful deductions/caps, probation/cause/unlawful termination, current instruments and exact interpretation of all qualifying cases.

### REG-AE-007 — Federal privacy scope, basis and processing controls

- **Jurisdiction/applicability:** L2 Article 2, including specified in/out-of-state processing and exclusions; special-law free zones and certain health/banking/government data are not automatically covered by this general law.
- **Official source:** L2 Articles 2, 4–8 and 20; L3 overview. Article 4 includes employment/social-security, contractual and legal-obligation exceptions to the general consent rule. Article 5 addresses purpose, minimization, accuracy/security; Articles 7/8 specify controller/processor responsibilities.
- **Effective date:** 2 January 2022 per official law page; implementing rules/compliance transition under Article 29 and current supervisory guidance need legal confirmation.
- **Business behavior:** determine processing purpose/basis and controller/processor role, minimize fields/recipients, provide required notices/rights and contractual processor instructions; do not collect blanket employee consent as a universal solution.
- **Data requirements:** purpose/basis/notice versions, role/recipient/processor registry, classification, retention/hold schedule, request and security evidence.
- **Validation requirements:** approved applicability assessment, encryption/access/security/recovery tests, scoped subject request handling and processing contracts; special-category/sector review before expansion.
- **Reporting requirements:** processing/security/rights-request records and regulator evidence when applicable; no presumed filing exemption from being a small SaaS company.
- **Outstanding legal review:** executive rules, DPO/DPIA triggers, special-data obligations, role allocation among agency/client/platform and current regulator mechanisms.

- **Effective date:** base 2 January 2022; current implementing timelines/mechanisms not established by this review.
- **Business behavior:** track awareness/incident evidence, notify appropriate controller/legal contact promptly under contract, assess regulator/subject duties and approved transfer mechanism; no assumed federal 72-hour SLA or blanket localization requirement.
- **Data requirements:** incident timestamps/categories/records/impact/mitigation/contact, transfer recipient/country/purpose/safeguards, retention authority and deletion/hold evidence.
- **Validation requirements:** breach tabletop, current approved obligation matrix, transfer/residency gate, erasure across artifacts/indexes/backups, no stale record resurrection.
- **Reporting requirements:** Article 9 required notice content and updates where applicable, access/deletion/transfer audit; legal lead approves actual notifications.
- **Outstanding legal review:** current executive regulations, competent authority channels, adequacy/contract/exception mechanisms, processor timing and applicable sector rules.

### REG-DIFC-002 — Working time, leave, termination and schemes

- **Jurisdiction/applicability:** employees covered by the verified-current DIFC regime once applicability/status is confirmed; qualifications in the published provisions matter.
- **Official source:** L5 Articles 19, 22–28, 62 and 66. - **Data requirements:** working time/calendars, service, contract/notice, salary/basic wage definitions, scheme start/exemption/contribution references, accrued leave and payment evidence.
- **Validation requirements:** legal-reviewed fixtures for work-time averaging, vacation qualification/carry-forward, notice/termination and pre/post-scheme service; block unreviewed scheme or worker cases.
- **Reporting requirements:** explainable rule/version, due entitlements/contributions and reconciled provider evidence; exported statements do not prove payment.
- **Outstanding legal review:** current legislation status, applicable qualifying scheme/DEWS details, pension/exemption rules, amendments, exact benefit bases and timing. No benefit engine approved by this report.

## LEG-003 — Architecture consequences and approval gates

1. Resolve employment and privacy regimes separately per legal entity/processing purpose, with explicit reviewer/effective date. Support multi-entity/multi-country records without one universal UAE switch.
2. Model statutory days, working days, wage/basic-wage definitions, currencies, scheme participation, service intervals, provider formats and notice/settlement deadlines as reviewed typed rule inputs. Never infer them from UI language, nationality alone or a customer address.
3. Keep statutory calculations/filings/payment services off in R1. A ready/start checklist is coordination evidence; employers/authorized parties retain employment, permit and payment responsibility.
4. Maintain a legal-source/change register and independent legal/engineering sign-off, including test fixtures and unsupported cases. DIFC metadata discrepancy, current MOHRE implementing requirements and the ADGM full-law gap are explicit blockers to affected pack approval.
5. Apply purpose/record-specific retention and transfer controls. The PRD defaults are service-design proposals; they do not override confirmed employee-file obligations or apply automatically to all candidate data.

Before any country pack is enabled, its REG records must gain confirmed full current sources, original/amendment effective dates, customer applicability, qualified interpretation, approved data/retention/transfer behavior, provider testing where relevant and executed TEST-F042/F043 plus affected payroll/leave/time/termination fixtures. This phase does not satisfy that activation gate.

Complete current executive/cabinet/ministerial resolutions for labour/WPS, agency licensing and alternative benefits; customer-specific non-financial free-zone rules; Arabic-first amendment review; DIFC current-force/date discrepancy and related regulations/scheme rules; ADGM full consolidated regulations/subordinate rules/current FAQs; full ADGM privacy text and federal implementing-rule/authority status; healthcare/financial/domestic-worker/government special regimes if entering those segments.
